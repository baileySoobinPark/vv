## Advance Notice of VASP Server Maintenance Schedule

VASPs must notify the VerifyVASP Operations Team of any scheduled server maintenance at least one week in advance. Notification can be made in the following ways:

### 1. Notification via Slack or Email

All VASPs participating in the VerifyVASP Alliance can communicate with the Operations Team through their dedicated Slack channel.\
If scheduled maintenance is planned, you must notify the Operations Team via Slack at least one week prior to the maintenance date.
If you prefer to use email, please send the maintenance details to the address below:

* Contact: [support@verifyvasp.com](mailto:support@verifyvasp.com)

<br />

### 2. Notification via VerifyVASP Console

You can also share your server maintenance schedule with member VASPs through the VerifyVASP Console.

1. Log in to the VerifyVASP Console and navigate to \[Members] > \[My Schedule] in the left-hand menu.
2. Click the \[Register] button to add your maintenance schedule. The list of maintenance schedules for all VASPs participating in the VerifyVASP Alliance will be displayed.
3. Enter the scheduled maintenance date and reason.
4. Once registered, the schedule will be visible to all VerifyVASP Alliance members.
