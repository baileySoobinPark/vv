### What should we do when VerifyVASP announces an update?

For significant updates, VerifyVASP provides advance notice and detailed information in the Change Log. Please apply the updates as required for your environment. Updates labeled as `Breaking Change` in the Change Log are critical and must be implemented promptly to ensure uninterrupted service.
