### Can additional accounts access the VV dashboard besides the one registered during onboarding?

Yes, additional accounts can access the VV dashboard.

Initial access authority is granted using the email address registered during the VV onboarding process. Once onboarding is complete, you can create up to five sub-accounts in the 'Account' tab of the VV dashboard, enabling access for those additional accounts.
